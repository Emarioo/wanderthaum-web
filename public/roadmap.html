A world in the making

Building something worth exploring.

Wanderthaum is an ambitious fantasy adventure built around exploration, freedom and discovery. This roadmap outlines the major milestones and features planned for its development.

Prototype

In Progress Q4 2026

Establish the foundations of Wanderthaum. Build a small interactive world and prove the core ideas behind exploration and interaction.

  • 3D world and camera controls
  • Entity placement and editing
  • Basic movement and interaction
  • Initial world rendering

Playable Foundation

Planned Q1 2027

Turn the prototype into a playable experience. Introduce the player, basic gameplay systems, and a world that can be explored.

  • Player character and movement
  • Inventory and equipment
  • Basic interaction system
  • Saving and loading
  • First explorable environment

The First Settlement

Planned Q2 2027

Bring life to the world with a small settlement. Meet its inhabitants, trade goods, purchase equipment, and prepare for journeys beyond its borders.

  • Village or adventurer's camp
  • NPCs and basic dialogue
  • Shops and trading
  • Currency and economy
  • Weapons and equipment

Adventure & Exploration

Planned Q3 2027

Give players reasons to venture into the wilderness. Discover creatures, gather resources, take risks, and return with something to show for it.

  • Creature encounters and combat
  • Gathering and crafting
  • Fishing and hunting
  • Loot and rewards
  • First quests and challenges

Magic & Discovery

Planned Q4 2027

Uncover the mysteries of magic. Learn spells, experiment with new abilities, and discover places where the ordinary rules of the world no longer apply.

  • Spellcasting foundations
  • Magic progression
  • Magical equipment
  • Academies and magical locations
  • Early dungeon exploration

A Living World

Planned Q1–Q2 2028

Make the world feel alive, even when the player is not directly involved. Improve the behaviour of creatures and inhabitants, and introduce more interesting activities and events.

  • Improved NPC behaviour and routines
  • More advanced creature AI
  • World events and activities
  • More quests and factions
  • Expanded settlements

Beyond the World

Planned Q3–Q4 2028

Discover that the world is larger than it first appeared. Ancient portals connect distant places and strange dimensions, opening the door to entirely new adventures.

  • Portals in major cities
  • New dimensions and environments
  • Unique creatures and resources
  • Dimension-specific discoveries

Shared Adventures

Planned 2029

The journey does not have to be taken alone. Bring friends into the world and explore, trade and adventure together.

  • Multiplayer foundations
  • Cooperative exploration
  • Shared world interactions
  • Player-to-player trading

World Refinement

Planned 2029+

Continue expanding and refining the experience. Make the world richer, more immersive and more enjoyable to spend time in.

  • More regions and settlements
  • Visual and environmental improvements
  • Sound and music
  • Performance and stability
  • Gameplay balance and refinement
